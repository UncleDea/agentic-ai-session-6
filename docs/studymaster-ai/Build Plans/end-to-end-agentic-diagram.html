12. Quote checker

Each important section includes a short quote. The quote stays when it is on the page, the main words appear on that page, and not or only matches the quote. Otherwise the pack says The source does not cover this.

13. Saved study pack

Model agent, after an answer

14. Grader

Scores the learner’s reply as correct, partial, or incorrect, and gives one hint. The attempt is saved. History and download do not call the model. Regenerating one section is the only later model call.
