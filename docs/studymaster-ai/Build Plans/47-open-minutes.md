# Build 47 — Open minutes

The picture is [47-open-minutes-diagram.png](47-open-minutes-diagram.png). Click that file to see the drawing.

This plan follows [01-studymaster-agent.md](01-studymaster-agent.md). Each saved study already says how many minutes it was given. This build shows that same line on the open pack.

## What this build does

Under the level line, the open pack names the minutes saved with that pack. A short pack says **5 minutes**. A longer pack says **20 minutes**. The Minutes box above the pack can still say 20. The line on the pack is the length that was saved.

Opening a saved study reads this computer and does not call OpenAI. The download stays the study text.

## Left alone

The reader, agents, three sentence checks, catalog, finder, pack size, and `.env` stay unchanged. The title stays **StudyMaster AI**.
