# Build 19 — Grounded sentences

The picture is [19-grounded-sentences-diagram.png](19-grounded-sentences-diagram.png). Click that file to see the drawing.

This plan follows [01-studymaster-agent.md](01-studymaster-agent.md). Each section already asks for a quote. This build leaves a sentence out of the pack the learner sees when that quote, or the sentence itself, is not on the page.

## What this build does

A sentence stays when all three are true:

1. Its quote is copied from the page.
2. Every main word appears somewhere on that page.
3. **Not** or **only** in the sentence matches the quote.

Otherwise the pack says **The source does not cover this.** The invented sentence and the bad quote are left out. A lesson question that fails is not offered for grading. The title stays **StudyMaster AI**.

## Left alone

The reader, catalog, progress box, Delete button, grader score, and `.env` stay unchanged.
