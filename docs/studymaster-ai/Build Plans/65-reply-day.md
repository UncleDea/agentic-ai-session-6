## What this build does

Under the question line, a saved study names the reply day. The 20-minute pack says **Reply day: October 25, 2026.** That is the same calendar day as **Saved October 25, 2026.** The two lines stay separate. An earlier reply stays off that line. A pack with no check yet leaves the line off. The hint and the reply stay off the list.

Showing the line reads this computer and does not call OpenAI.
