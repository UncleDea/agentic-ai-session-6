This plan follows [01-studymaster-agent.md](01-studymaster-agent.md). A pack with a question leads with that question.

## What this build does

On a pack that has at least one question, the page shows the first question and its answer box, then the lesson, then any later questions, then the study, lab, project, and runbook. The pack title and the left-out line stay above the first question. Download and the rewrite buttons stay at the bottom.

A pack with no questions keeps the section order it already has. Opening a saved study uses the same screen order and does not call OpenAI. The downloaded file keeps the stored order.

## Left alone

The three checks stay. Hidden answers stay hidden until **Check my answer**. The title stays **StudyMaster AI**.
