# Build 50 — Open pieces

The picture is [50-open-pieces-diagram.png](50-open-pieces-diagram.png). Click that file to see the drawing.

This plan follows [01-studymaster-agent.md](01-studymaster-agent.md). Each saved study already names the pieces it has. This build shows that same line on the open pack.

## What this build does

Under the day line, the open pack names the pieces saved with that pack. A full pack says **Study, lesson, lab, project, runbook**. A short pack says **Study, lesson**. A section that failed stays off the line.

Opening a saved study reads this computer and does not call OpenAI. The download stays the study text.

## Left alone

The reader, agents, three sentence checks, catalog, finder, pack size, and `.env` stay unchanged. The title stays **StudyMaster AI**.
